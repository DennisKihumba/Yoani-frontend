About Yoani Springs Academy

Building tomorrow’s leaders, one child at a time

Our Story

Founded in 2024, Yoani Springs Academy began with a simple yet powerful belief: every child deserves an education that ignites curiosity and builds character. What started as a small kindergarten that has grown into a thriving primary school in Makueni County and beyond.

Our location in Salama Town, behind Tangu Springs Hotel, offers a serene environment where children feel safe, inspired, and motivated to excel. We are proud to be a registered CBE institution, fully equipped to deliver the Competency‑Based Education while maintaining our own tradition of holistic growth.

At Yoani Springs, we don’t just teach – we mentor, we guide, and we celebrate every milestone a child achieves.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide a nurturing, learner‑centered environment that empowers every student with knowledge, skills, and values to excel academically and become responsible leaders.

🌟

Our Vision

To be the leading primary school in Kenya, recognized for academic excellence, character formation, and innovative teaching.
