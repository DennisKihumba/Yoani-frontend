How admission works

Five simple steps from your first visit to your child's first day.

  1. Get in touch

    Book a visit online or come to the school to learn about our programs, fees and requirements.

  2. Apply

    Fill in the admission form and hand it in with the documents below.

  3. Assessment and interview

    Your child takes a short, age-appropriate assessment and we meet you briefly, so we can place them in the right class.

  4. Receive your offer

    You get an admission offer with details on fees, uniform, transport and start dates.

  5. Enrol

    Once fees are paid and documents are in, your child is officially enrolled.

Questions parents ask

How much are the fees?

For 2026, fees per term are KES 8,250 for Pre-Primary, KES 9,250 for Lower Primary and KES 9,750 for Upper Primary. New learners pay a one-off admission fee of KES 1,000. See the full breakdown.

Which classes do you take?

Playgroup through to Grade 6, following the Competency-Based Education (CBE) curriculum.

How big are the classes?

No class has more than 20 learners.

What happens on an interview day?

Your child takes a short, age-appropriate assessment and we have a brief chat with you, so we can place your child in the right class. Interviews start at 9:00 am.

Do you offer school transport?

Yes. Transport is priced by distance from the school, starting at KES 3,000 a term. Routes and schedules are in the transport guide (PDF).

Can I visit before applying?

Yes, and we recommend it. Book a private tour above, or call us on 0725 906 416.
