Our Academic Journey

Nurturing curious minds from their first steps through upper primary

Baby Care & Kindergarten

A warm, play‑based environment where children develop social, emotional, and early academic skills through structured play, storytelling, and creative activities.

  • Qualified early‑years educators
  • Even Child‑to‑teacher ratio
  • Indoor & outdoor learning zones
  • Focus on language, numeracy, and motor skills

Lower Primary (Grade 1–3)

Building a strong foundation in literacy, numeracy, and scientific thinking. Our interactive methods encourage curiosity and confidence in every child.

  • CBE curriculum fully implemented
  • Integration of local Kenyan context
  • Weekly library and computer sessions
  • Co‑curricular: music, art, sports

Upper Primary (Grade 4–6)

Preparation for independent learning and leadership. Students master core subjects, develop critical thinking, and engage in community projects.

  • Advanced CBC pathways
  • STEM projects and science fairs
  • Leadership clubs and debates
  • Career guidance and mentorship
