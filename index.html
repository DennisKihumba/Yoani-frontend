Igniting a Passion for Knowledge

Yoani Springs Academy is located in Salama Town, Makueni County, behind Tangu Springs Hotel, 300m from Salama town. The school provides a nurturing environment that empowers learners to take an active role in their education.

We blend academic rigor with character development, ensuring every child discovers their unique potential and grows into a responsible leader.
